Welcome

I am an economist at the Economic Research Institute of the Bank of Korea, where I work on macroeconomics, monetary economics, and public finance.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Sungkyunkwan University in 2022. Before joining the Bank of Korea, I worked at the Hyundai Research Institute and the Korea Institute of Finance, and I continue to combine academic research with policy analysis.
